# Privacy

KhataGo is designed for local, personal record keeping.

- Financial data is stored locally in the app's Room database.
- Profile preferences are stored locally with DataStore.
- The app does not request internet access and has no analytics, advertising or tracking SDK.
- There is no mandatory account, sign-in or cloud sync.
- Local notifications are generated by Android WorkManager and contain only a short due-count.
- When you create a backup, export or share a file, that copy leaves the app only because you explicitly chose to do so.
- Exported JSON and CSV files are not encrypted by KhataGo. Keep them in a protected location and delete copies you no longer need.
- Android system backups and file providers may have their own behavior; the app sets `allowBackup=false` and does not claim protection beyond that setting.

KhataGo provides record keeping, not professional financial, lending, tax or legal advice. Always verify entries against original statements and agreements.
